✦ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What makes Gurukul Here different? +
We focus on 1:1 and micro-batch (1:3) teaching — your child gets 100% attention, not 1.6% like in a 60-student mass batch. Every doubt is cleared and every student truly seen.
Which boards & exams do you cover? +
CBSE, ICSE, GSEB, and NCERT curricula. Specialized programs for NEET, JEE Mains & Advanced, Olympiads, and Foundation courses for grades 9–12.
How are classes conducted? +
Live online via Zoom or Google Meet. Students get digital study materials, homework, and recorded sessions. Our student portal gives access to notes and schedules anytime.
Can I get a free demo class? +
Yes! We offer a free 30-minute diagnostic session to understand your child's needs, assess their level, and show you our teaching approach. No strings attached.
What's the fee structure? +
Fees vary by course and batch type (1:1 or 1:3). Contact us at +91 84453 17131 for a personalized quote, or book a free demo to discuss your child's needs.
How do you track student progress? +
Weekly assessments, detailed progress reports, and monthly parent-teacher meetings. Our student portal gives real-time access to homework, attendance, and performance analytics.
